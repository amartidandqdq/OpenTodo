## Why
Switching workspaces requires opening a menu, and the current space is easy to miss (upstream issue 780).

## What Changes
Add direct workspace buttons and a clear current indicator in the sidebar while retaining the management menu.

## Capabilities
### Modified Capabilities
- `workspaces`: direct workspace selection in the sidebar.

## Impact
Client sidebar only; no API or data changes. Advances workspace usability; no specific ROADMAP item. Global theme tint and numbered shortcuts are out of scope.
